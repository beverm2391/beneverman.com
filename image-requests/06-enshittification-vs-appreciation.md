# Figure 06, enshittification vs appreciation

`06-enshittification-vs-appreciation.png`

The hook act's axis, drawn: closed technology loses value in your hands over
time, open technology gains it. The audience has lived the downward curve
(Uber, Instagram, their bed); the figure's job is to make the upward curve —
which they have never been offered — look like the obvious other half of the
same picture. It backs the "Closed vs open technology" slide.

## Composition

A plot, same drafting register as figures 01 and 05.

- X axis: `TIME YOU OWN IT`
- Y axis: `VALUE TO YOU`

Two curves leaving the origin region together:

- `CLOSED`, rising briefly (the honeymoon), then bending down and continuing
  to decay. Label the curve `ENSHITTIFICATION` along its falling section.
- `OPEN`, rising steadily and continuing to rise. Label the curve
  `APPRECIATION` along its rising section.

The two labels are the argument, so they sit on the curves themselves in mono,
not in a legend.

Small tick annotations, two or three per curve, naming what moves it, in the
smallest mono size:

- On the closed curve's fall: `PRICE HIKES`, `ADS`, `FEATURES REMOVED`.
- On the open curve's rise: `COMMUNITY SPEEDUPS`, `NEW TOOLS`, `RUNS ON MORE
  HARDWARE`.

The gap between the curves at the right edge is the point. Mark it with a
dotted bracket labelled `WHAT OWNING IT IS WORTH`.

The closed curve's decay carries the accent color; it is the harm, and the
only accent in the figure. Axes quieter than the curves, curves in neutral
ink, labels in working ink, per the figure 05 revision notes.
