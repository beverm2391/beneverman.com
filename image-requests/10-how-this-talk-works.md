# Figure 10, how this talk works

`10-how-this-talk-works.png`

The map of the deck itself, shown right after the title: concepts run left to
right at equal scope, and optional dives stack downward into increasing
technical depth. The audience learns the mechanic as a promise — we only dig
where you want to. A code-native placeholder currently holds the slide; this
figure replaces it if it reads better.

## Composition

A flat schematic grid of small slide rectangles:

- A spine row of seven rectangles in neutral ink, labelled in mono:
  `INTRO`, `CHATBOTS`, `UBER`, `WHY?`, `OPEN`, `HOW`, `END`.
- Dive stacks in working ink descending under three of them, fading with
  depth: under `CHATBOTS` — `MATH`, `FIGS`; under `WHY?` — `ECON`,
  `FRIEDMAN`, `FIG`; under `HOW` — `MODELS`.
- A long thin arrow across the top labelled `CONCEPTS`; a thin arrow down the
  left labelled `DEPTH`.

No accent color. Nothing here constrains anyone — the point is the opposite.
