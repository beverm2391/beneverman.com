# Figure 05, retention diverges from value

`05-retention-diverges-from-value.png`

The mental model behind the incentives half of the talk. Retention is a proxy
for user value. While the proxy tracks the thing, everyone is fine. Once
optimization pressure lands on the proxy, the two separate, and everything past
that point is tuned for the lab rather than the user.

The figure has to make the split visible and name the two forces that cause it,
because the talk's examples divide between them.

## Composition

A plot, same drafting register as figure 01.

- X axis: `OPTIMIZATION PRESSURE`
- Y axis: unlabeled, or `OUTCOME`

Two curves leaving the origin together:

- `RETENTION`, continuing to climb.
- `YOUR VALUE`, tracking retention closely at first, then bending away and
  flattening or declining.

Mark the point where they separate with a small vertical tick and the label
`THE PROXY STOPS TRACKING`.

Between the two curves, past the split, shade the widening gap lightly and
label it `OPTIMIZED FOR THEM, NOT YOU`. This gap is the harm, so it carries the
accent color. It is the only accent in the figure.

## The two forces

Two callouts on the gap, drawn as arrows acting on the value curve from
opposite directions:

- An arrow pushing up on retention labeled `PULL, COME BACK`, with a small
  mono list beneath: `SYCOPHANCY / ENGAGEMENT LOOPS`. This force adds what
  retains.
- An arrow pressing down on value labeled `PUSH, DO NOT GET SUED`, with a
  small mono list beneath: `CLASSIFIERS / SOFT REFUSAL`. This force removes
  what risks.

The opposition matters: one adds what retains, the other subtracts what risks,
and both widen the same gap.

## Notes

Keep the curves in neutral ink and the labels in working ink, so the accent on
the gap is the only warm thing on the page. It should be the first thing the
eye lands on.
