# Figure 07, the subscription airbag

`07-subscription-airbag.png`

The peak receipt of the closed-tech-goes-wrong beat: a motorcycle airbag vest
whose crash detection is disabled remotely for nonpayment. The audience has
just seen prices and ads; this is the escalation to safety equipment with a
kill switch. The figure must make one thing legible instantly: the hardware is
on your body, but the decision to protect you is on someone else's server.

## Composition

An isometric technical drawing of a motorcycle airbag vest, drawn like an
exploded product schematic.

- The vest body in working ink with pale fill, drawn complete and functional:
  collar, panels, the folded airbag layer indicated.
- The detection module drawn slightly exploded out of its chest pocket, a
  small rectangular unit, connected to the vest by a thin leader.
- From the module, a dashed leader line rises to a small cloud or server
  glyph. On this line sits an open switch, drawn in circuit-diagram style —
  the contact visibly disconnected.
- The switch is the accent element, the only accent in the figure. Label it
  `PAYMENT`.
- Label the module `CRASH DETECTION`, and along the dashed line, in the
  smallest mono size: `STOPS DETECTING CRASHES UNTIL PAYMENT IS RESUMED` —
  the vendor's own words.

The vest must read as intact and yours; nothing about the garment is broken.
Only the switch, elsewhere, is open. That contrast is the argument.
