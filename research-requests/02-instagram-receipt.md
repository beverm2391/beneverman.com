# Research 02, the Instagram receipt

Feeds the `receipt-instagram` slide. Same shape as the Uber receipt: a felt
memory (the chronological friends feed), dated hinges, and the accented now.
The slide's spoken argument is Meta's retention optimization, so the hinges
must be the moments the feed stopped serving the user.

## Deliverables

1. **The chronological era, dated.** When did Instagram run a purely
   chronological friends feed, and when exactly did the algorithmic feed
   replace it? The slide currently guesses 2010–2015 with the switch in 2016.
2. **The Reels hinge, dated.** Launch date, and whether there is a citable
   moment where recommended/suggested content overtook followed content in
   the default feed.
3. **Major ad-load changes.** Any dated, citable steps in ad or suggested-post
   density — first ads, ad-load increases, the "make Instagram Instagram
   again" backlash and Meta's response.
4. **The Doctorow TikTok enshittification article** (Wired, Jan 2023): pull
   anything that dates or names the platform lifecycle stages usefully for
   this receipt or the deck's axis slide. It stays uncited until Ben reads
   it — quote sparingly and attach the claim it would support.

## Output

- Facts with sources per claim; Ben reads before anything is cited.
- Corrected dates go straight into the `receipt-instagram` marks in
  `components/presentations/open-weights/slides.tsx`.
- Append findings to this file.
